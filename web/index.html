Sightread.

Audio description has to fit in the silence between two lines of dialogue, to the millisecond. Sightread measures those silences in the actual audio, writes a line for each one, renders it, then measures the rendered audio again and rejects the takes that overrun. Nothing is accepted because a model said it would fit.

The takes that were thrown out

These are the lines Gemini wrote that did not survive being measured. Each one was rendered to real speech, and each one ran past the end of the silence it was written for. Play any of them against the silence length beside it. A model reporting success cannot produce this list.

Every take, in order, per silence

One row per attempt. The track is the measured silence; the shaded band inside it is what is usable after 250ms of headroom is kept at each end so the line never clips the dialogue around it. A bar that runs past the band was rejected, and you can play it.

The character budget came from measuring, not from a convention

Subtitle practice reads at about 14 characters per second, so that is what the first version assumed. The rendered audio disagreed.

The graph, and the step that cannot be skipped

A Google ADK 2.8 Workflow. Two nodes call Gemini, and neither of them produces a number that reaches a verdict: the planner chooses which silences deserve a listener's attention, and conform gets the line written and spoken, but ffprobe decides whether it fit. verify then re-measures every accepted WAV from disk and recomputes its verdict independently, so a conform step that reported a fit it never measured would surface here as a disagreement.

Built with IBM Bob

Bob Shell 2.0.2, driven over the Agent Client Protocol from tools/bob_acp.py. Each session's transcript is in the repository and holds the byte-for-byte content Bob wrote for every file, alongside the session id that Bob's own task ledger records independently.

Source: Night Tide (1961), public domain, from archive.org. Gap boundaries are ffmpeg silencedetect; rendered durations are ffprobe on the WAV that came back. Descriptions from Gemini 2.5 Flash on Vertex AI, speech from Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS, voice Kore. Orchestrated as a Google ADK 2.8 Workflow.
